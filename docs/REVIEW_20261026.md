## Architectural advice accepted or qualified

All three reviewers were asked about security, correctness, performance, shared PBX resources, compatibility, feature gaps, usability, bilingual/RTL layout and motion. New-module advice is preventive, not a list of proven P0 vulnerabilities. Shared admission control, bounded outboxes, explicit hook ordering, number-as-untrusted-claim, current artifact permissions and reconciliation of unknown external outcomes are required design constraints.

Reviewer suggestions to automatically expire/delete records are not applied: the owner requested no automatic deletion. A claimed exactly-once external telephone effect is also not promised; ambiguous Originate results require reconciliation, rather than a blind retry.
